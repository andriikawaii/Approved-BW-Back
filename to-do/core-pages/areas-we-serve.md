H1:
Areas We Serve: Fairfield County and New Haven County, CT

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Intro
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

We provide home remodeling services throughout [Fairfield County](/fairfield-county/) and [New Haven County](/new-haven-county/), Connecticut. We are headquartered in [Orange, CT](/new-haven-county/orange-ct/), which puts us well-positioned to reach homeowners in both counties without the delays that come from a contractor working too far from their base. Whether your project is in coastal Fairfield County or anywhere across New Haven County, you're within our service area.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Fairfield County
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H2: Fairfield County

Fairfield County has some of the most varied residential housing stock in Connecticut. You'll find century-old colonials and Tudors alongside mid-century split-levels, post-war cape cods, and waterfront homes along the Long Island Sound coast. The towns range from the dense urban character of Stamford and Norwalk to the quieter, more rural feel of Ridgefield and New Canaan, and the housing in each reflects that variety. We work in all of it: aging kitchens in older homes, dated bathrooms in mid-century ranches, unfinished basements in colonials, and whole-floor renovations in larger properties. Knowing the construction patterns common to Fairfield County homes and the permit requirements in each town is part of what we bring to every project here.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
New Haven County
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H2: New Haven County

New Haven County covers a wide range of neighborhoods and housing types: coastal towns like Branford, Guilford, and Madison with their mix of seasonal and year-round homes; historic neighborhoods in New Haven and Hamden with older housing stock that rewards careful renovation; and inland suburban communities like Woodbridge and Milford where colonial and cape-style homes were built out across several decades. Orange is our home base, and it's where we manage day-to-day operations for New Haven County work. We know the building departments, the permit timelines, and the types of homes common throughout the county.

Towns we serve in New Haven County:

Our Services in Connecticut
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H2: Our Services in Connecticut

Across both counties, we offer the same 11 remodeling services: kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, basement finishing, flooring installation, home additions, interior painting, interior carpentry, attic conversions, decks and porches, remodeling design and planning, and comfort and accessibility remodeling. The service is the same whether you're in Greenwich or Guilford, Westport or Woodbridge. For full details on each service, visit our [services page](/services/).


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
How We Serve Both Counties
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H2: How We Serve Both Counties

Serving two counties well requires being genuinely positioned to do it. We're not stretching to reach areas outside our range; our Orange, CT office handles New Haven County projects directly, and our Fairfield County team is based to cover the towns on that side efficiently. Both teams operate under the same license, the same process, the same quality standards, and the same warranty. When you work with us, you're working with one company, not a franchise or a referral network. The crew that shows up at your home is our crew. The project manager you talk to on day one is the one you'll talk to on the last day.

If you're not sure which county you're in or which phone number to use, [contact us](/contact/) and we'll sort it out. Both numbers reach our team.
