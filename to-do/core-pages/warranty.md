H1: Our Warranty

We stand behind the work we complete. This page explains exactly what is covered, for how long, and what to do if something needs attention after your project is done.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION: Workmanship Warranty
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H2: Workmanship Warranty

BuiltWell provides a 2-year workmanship warranty on all labor performed on your project, covering defects in how the work was installed or built. If something we installed fails because of how it was installed, we come back and correct it at no charge.

This warranty is straightforward. If something we built or installed doesn't hold up because of how it was done, we return and make it right. No charge, no argument about whether it qualifies.

What the workmanship warranty covers:

- Defects in installation: tile that lifts, trim that separates, fixtures that fail because of how they were set, framing issues, or any other condition that traces back to how the work was performed
- Any labor-related failure on your project within 2 years of project completion

What the workmanship warranty does not cover:

- Normal wear over time, including floors, painted finishes, and grout that show age through regular use
- Damage caused by the homeowner, other contractors, or third parties after the project is complete
- Water intrusion from homeowner-controlled sources such as a roof leak, HVAC failure, or exterior drainage issue that the homeowner is responsible for maintaining
- Modifications made to the completed work by anyone other than BuiltWell
- Pre-existing conditions that were identified and disclosed at the time of the project, such as a moisture issue, foundation concern, or existing structural condition noted in the proposal

The warranty applies to the original project location and the original homeowner.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION: Manufacturer and Product Warranties
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H2: Manufacturer and Product Warranties

Many materials and products we install carry their own manufacturer warranties, which are separate from and in addition to our workmanship warranty, meaning you are covered by both.

Cabinets, countertops, flooring, fixtures, and appliances each carry warranties that vary by manufacturer and product line. These warranties are provided directly by the manufacturer and cover product defects rather than installation issues.

At project completion, we provide documentation for all applicable product warranties. Where a manufacturer requires product registration to activate warranty coverage, we register the product on your behalf.

SECTION: What Is Not Covered
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H2: What Is Not Covered

This section is written plainly so there's no confusion later. The workmanship warranty covers how we installed the work. It does not cover everything that can happen to a home after a project is complete.

The following are not covered:

- Normal wear over time. Floors get scratched, finishes fade, grout discolors with years of use. That is expected maintenance, not a defect.
- Damage from misuse or outside causes. If a fixture is damaged by impact, a floor is damaged by a water leak unrelated to the project, or any part of the finished work is altered by someone other than our team, that falls outside the warranty.
- Pre-existing conditions. If a condition was present before construction began and was disclosed to you in writing at the time of the project, we are not responsible for how that condition affects the finished work over time. We document pre-existing conditions in the project proposal precisely to avoid this ambiguity.
- Deferred maintenance. If a home system that affects the project area is not maintained after completion, any resulting damage to our work is not covered.

If you're not sure whether something qualifies, contact us and we'll give you a direct answer.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION: How to Request Warranty Service
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H2: How to Request Warranty Service

If you believe something we installed needs attention under warranty, contact us directly; we respond within one business day.

Here is the process:

1. Contact us by phone or through the contact form at /contact/. Describe what you're seeing.
2. We'll confirm receipt and schedule a visit to assess the condition firsthand.
3. If the issue falls within workmanship warranty coverage, we schedule the repair and complete it at no cost to you.
4. If the issue is not covered under the workmanship warranty, we'll tell you clearly why, and we can discuss repair options if you'd like to proceed.

There's no complicated claim process. You call or write, we look at it, and we handle it if it's ours to handle.
