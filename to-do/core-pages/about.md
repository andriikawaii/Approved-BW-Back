H2: Who We Are

We're a licensed home remodeling contractor based in Connecticut, serving homeowners throughout Fairfield County and New Haven County. We do kitchens, bathrooms, basements, additions, flooring, painting, carpentry, and more: the full range of interior residential remodeling.

This company was built around a straightforward idea: remodeling doesn't have to be chaotic. Too many homeowners have been through it: the contractor who went quiet, the project that stretched well past the promised date, the finish work that never quite got finished. We've heard those stories enough times that we built our entire operation to prevent them.

We communicate before problems become surprises. We keep the schedule we commit to. We complete what we start. Those aren't aspirations; they're the standard we hold ourselves to on every project, for every client. See our services or read our case studies to understand how we work in practice.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION: Our Team
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H2: Our Team

H3: David Magenishvili, Owner and Founder

David started BuiltWell CT because he saw a gap between what homeowners needed from a remodeling contractor and what most of them actually got. The technical work wasn't the hard part; it was the reliability, the communication, the follow-through. He built this company around the conviction that those things don't have to be rare.

He runs a tight operation. Schedules are kept. Proposals are written clearly. Clients get answers when they ask questions. David is involved in every project from estimate to final walkthrough, and the standards he sets for the company are the same standards he holds himself to. He's not interested in volume for its own sake; he's interested in doing the work right and building a reputation that earns the next call.

H3: Malkhazi Tvalabeishvili, Lead Foreman

Malkhazi leads the field crews on every project. He's the person on the ground making sure the work matches the plan: that framing is square, tile is set correctly, trim is installed with care, and nothing gets rushed at the finish line because a deadline is near. He sets the pace and the standard for every crew member working under him.

What you'll notice on a BuiltWell CT job site is that things are done in order, the site is clean at the end of every day, and the crew treats your home with respect. That's Malkhazi's influence. Craftsmanship is his standard, not a selling point.

SECTION: How We Work
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H2: How We Work

Every project follows the same five-step process: consultation, planning, selections, build, and walkthrough. The structure exists so nothing falls through the cracks and you always know where things stand. Learn more about our full process.

Before we start construction, you have a written proposal in hand: scope, timeline, and cost, spelled out clearly. No vague estimates, no surprises on the back end. During construction, you receive daily updates. Not a weekly check-in, not a call when something goes wrong, but daily communication so you're never left wondering what happened on your job site today.

The site is cleaned every evening before the crew leaves. Tools are put away. Materials are stored. Dust and debris don't accumulate in your home. We've found that a clean job site isn't just about courtesy; it reflects how organized the work is, and organized work finishes on schedule.

When the project is complete, we walk through everything together. If something isn't right, we don't close out until it is.

SECTION: Our License and Insurance
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H2: Our License and Insurance

We hold CT Home Improvement Contractor License #0668405, issued by the State of Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection. We carry general liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage.

Here's what that means for you in practical terms. If a worker is injured on your property during construction, workers' compensation covers it, so you're not exposed to a liability claim. If your property is damaged during the project, our general liability insurance covers the loss. You're not left holding the problem.

Connecticut law requires contractors performing home improvement work above a threshold to be licensed. That license is tied to our legal name, Legacy of Clean LLC d/b/a BuiltWell CT, and it's verifiable through the state. When you hire an unlicensed contractor, you have fewer legal protections if something goes wrong. When you hire us, you have documentation, recourse, and a contractor who has put their license on the line.

We also require the same standard from any licensed subcontractors we bring in when specialty trades are needed. You get one point of contact and one accountable party.
