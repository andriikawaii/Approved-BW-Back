H1: Our Completed Projects

We're a licensed home remodeling contractor serving Fairfield County and New Haven County, Connecticut. The projects shown here are from actual completed jobs in Connecticut homes: kitchens, bathrooms, basements, and flooring work across communities in both counties. No renderings, no stock photography, no projects from other markets. Every photo is from a real home we worked in.

This portfolio reflects the range of project types and scopes we handle, from straightforward bathroom refreshes to full kitchen gut-and-rebuilds with layout changes. If you want to go deeper on any project, the case studies linked throughout this page include full project details, timelines, and client testimonials.

H2: Kitchen Remodeling Projects

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H3: New Canaan: Full Kitchen Remodel with Flooring (Featured Case Study)

Scope: Complete gut renovation of a 240-square-foot kitchen in a colonial-style home. Work included cabinet removal and replacement with custom inset cabinetry, quartz countertops, new tile backsplash, recessed and under-cabinet lighting, and replacement of all appliances. Hardwood flooring was extended through the adjacent dining area to create visual continuity. The project required coordination with electricians for panel capacity and new dedicated circuits for appliances. Total timeline: 10 weeks.

Read the full case study: /case-studies/kitchen-remodeling-new-canaan/

H3: Milford: Kitchen Remodel with LVP Flooring (Featured Case Study)

Scope: Full kitchen remodel in a 1960s ranch-style home in Milford. New semi-custom cabinets, laminate countertops replaced with quartz, tile backsplash, updated lighting, and new LVP flooring installed throughout the kitchen and into the adjoining living area. The client remained in the home throughout construction. Total timeline: 6 weeks.

H3: Darien: Kitchen Update with Layout Modification

Scope: Mid-range kitchen update in a Darien colonial. The project involved removing a non-load-bearing peninsula wall to open the kitchen to the living area, installing new semi-custom cabinetry, quartz countertops, and tile backsplash. Plumbing relocation was required for the island sink. Rock ledge conditions in the crawlspace were documented but did not affect the scope. Total timeline: 8 weeks.

H3: Westport: Kitchen Refresh with New Countertops and Appliances

Scope: Targeted kitchen update in a 1970s colonial in Westport. Existing cabinets were refinished and fitted with new hardware. Laminate countertops replaced with quartz. New stainless steel appliances installed. Tile backsplash added. Salt air exposure from coastal proximity was factored into material selection for hardware and fixtures. Total timeline: 3 weeks.

H2: Bathroom Remodeling Projects

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H3: Westport: Full Primary Bathroom Remodel (Featured Case Study)

Scope: Complete gut renovation of a primary bathroom in a Westport colonial. The project included full tile removal, new large-format floor tile, a custom walk-in shower with bench and niche, a new double vanity with undermount sinks, updated lighting, and a soaking tub. All plumbing rough-in was retained in its existing location to avoid floor penetrations. Total timeline: 4 weeks.

Scope: Full bathroom renovation completed as part of a larger multi-room restoration in Hamden following water damage. Work included replacing all drywall, new tile throughout, new vanity, toilet, fixtures, and lighting. The bathroom was coordinated alongside flooring and painting scopes across multiple rooms. Total timeline for full project: 5 weeks.

H2: Basement Finishing Projects

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H3: Darien: Basement Finishing with Egress Window (Featured Case Study)

Scope: Full basement finishing project in a Darien colonial: 850 square feet converted from unfinished utility space to usable living area. Work included framing, insulation, drywall, LVP flooring, a new egress window to meet code requirements, and rough-in for a future bathroom. Rock ledge conditions below the foundation were documented during the planning phase and accounted for in the scope. Permit pulled and managed by BuiltWell. Total timeline: 6 weeks.

H3: Orange: Basement Conversion to Rec Room and Home Office

Scope: 700-square-foot unfinished basement converted to a rec room, a dedicated home office, and a utility room partition. Work included framing, drywall, LVP flooring throughout, recessed lighting, and electrical for dedicated office circuits. Moisture assessment was completed prior to framing; no active water intrusion was found. Permit pulled and managed by BuiltWell from our Orange office. Total timeline: 5 weeks.

H3: Norwalk: Basement Finishing with Full Bathroom

Scope: Mid-range basement finishing project in a Norwalk home. Scope included framing, insulation, drywall, LVP flooring, recessed lighting, and a full bathroom with tiled shower, vanity, and toilet. Plumbing rough-in for the basement bathroom required coordination with the town building department for separate plumbing and building permits. Total timeline: 7 weeks.

Image recommendation:
builtwell-ct-basement-bathroom-norwalk.webp — Alt: Finished basement with full bathroom and LVP flooring in Norwalk CT by BuiltWell CT

H3: Guilford: Basement Finishing with Moisture Mitigation

Scope: Basement finishing project in a Guilford home with coastal proximity. Prior to framing, a drainage board system was installed along the foundation perimeter to address hydrostatic pressure typical in Long Island Sound-adjacent areas. Work then proceeded with standard framing, insulation, drywall, and tile flooring selected for moisture tolerance. Total timeline: 6.5 weeks.

H2: Flooring Projects

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H3: Milford: LVP Flooring Throughout Kitchen and Living Area

Scope: LVP flooring installation throughout the kitchen, dining area, and living room in a Milford ranch home, completed as part of a full kitchen remodel. Existing vinyl flooring removed, subfloor leveled, and 1,100 square feet of LVP installed in a continuous run across rooms. Transitions to existing carpet in the bedrooms were set at doorways. Total timeline: 3 days for flooring scope.

Image recommendation:
builtwell-ct-lvp-flooring-milford.webp — Alt: LVP flooring installed throughout kitchen and living room in Milford CT by BuiltWell CT

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H3: Darien: LVP Flooring in Finished Basement

Scope: 850 square feet of LVP flooring installed in a finished basement in Darien, completed as part of the full basement finishing project. LVP was selected for its moisture tolerance given the below-grade environment and Darien's rock ledge and high water table conditions. Flooring was installed after framing and drywall were complete and the space was dry. Total timeline: 2 days for flooring scope.

Scope: Solid hardwood flooring installed in a New Canaan kitchen and extended to match existing hardwood in the adjacent dining room, completed as part of a full kitchen remodel. New flooring was site-finished after installation to match the existing sheen and tone. Transition between old and new flooring was planned to fall at a doorway threshold. Total timeline: 4 days for flooring scope.

Scope: Full flooring replacement across first floor of a Hamden colonial following water damage, completed as part of a whole-home restoration. Damaged hardwood and subfloor sections removed, subfloor repaired, and new engineered hardwood installed in the living room, dining room, and hallway. LVP installed in the kitchen.
