Join the BuiltWell Team

BuiltWell is a Connecticut home remodeling company built around the idea that good contractors communicate, arrive on time, and complete what they start. We are looking for skilled people who hold themselves to the same standard.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION: Who We Are Looking For
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H2: Who We Are Looking For

We are not looking for someone who needs to be managed closely or reminded to show up. We are looking for tradespeople who take their work seriously: people who care whether a tile is set correctly, whether a trim joint is tight, whether the homeowner's floor was protected before someone walked across it.

The person we want on our crew does the following without being asked: arrives when scheduled, communicates if something has changed, keeps the job site clean throughout the day, and takes ownership when something goes wrong rather than looking for a way around it. Those traits are harder to find than the technical skills, and they matter just as much.

We are typically hiring for:

- Experienced carpenters and finish carpenters
- Tile setters
- Flooring installers (hardwood, LVP, and tile)
- Project leads and lead carpenters
- Operations coordinator (scheduling, estimates, and customer communication)

If your trade is not on this list but you have a track record of showing up reliably and doing good work, reach out anyway. We are always willing to talk to someone who fits how we operate.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION: What We Offer
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H2: What We Offer

We will be straightforward about what working here actually looks like.

We book 3 to 4 months out consistently. That means steady, predictable work, not a burst of activity followed by slow weeks. If you have been living with the uncertainty of feast-or-famine scheduling, that is something we can offer stability around.

Pay is fair and it is paid on time. We do not have complicated arrangements or delayed settlements. You do the work, you get paid on schedule.

We work in Fairfield County and New Haven County. No long drives to unfamiliar areas, no overnight travel, no projects two states away. The work is local.

You are treated as a professional. We are owner-operated, which means decisions get made quickly, problems get addressed directly, and there is no chain of people to pass a question through before something gets resolved. If something on a job site needs attention, it gets attention.
