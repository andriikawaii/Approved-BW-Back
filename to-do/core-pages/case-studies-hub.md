H2: Whole-Home Restoration in Hamden, CT

Location: Hamden, CT (New Haven County)
Services: Flooring, Interior Painting, Bathroom Remodeling, Drywall
Timeline: 5 weeks
Contact: (203) 466-9148

The Martin family faced significant interior damage throughout their Hamden home, damage that touched multiple surfaces and required coordination across flooring, painting, drywall, and bathroom trades simultaneously. Rather than managing multiple contractors with separate schedules and separate agreements, they brought everything under a single contract with us. We coordinated all four trades under one project schedule, which meant the Martins dealt with one point of contact, received one daily update, and had one crew responsible for the finished result.

The project ran five weeks from construction start to final walkthrough. All four scopes were sequenced to avoid trade conflicts: drywall and framing first, then bathroom rough-in, then flooring, then paint, with no overlap that would require rework. The home was returned to full use in five weeks, with all work permitted, inspected, and complete.

"We were devastated when we saw the damage. BuiltWell took everything off our plate." - The Martins, Hamden

H2: Basement Finishing in Darien, CT

Location: Darien, CT (Fairfield County)
Services: Basement Finishing, Flooring, Egress Window Installation
Timeline: 6 weeks | 850 square feet
Contact: (203) 919-9616

Steve R.'s Darien home had an 850-square-foot unfinished basement that had been used for storage since the family moved in. The decision to finish it came alongside a desire to add usable square footage for a home office and recreation area. The project included full basement finishing (framing, insulation, drywall, electrical, and ceiling), LVP flooring throughout, and egress window installation to meet Connecticut building code requirements for the intended use of the space.

We assessed moisture conditions before any work began. Moisture levels were acceptable, and we moved forward with the finish plan without requiring remediation. Permits were pulled and approved, and construction began on schedule. The egress window was installed and inspected as part of the permitted scope. The finished basement added meaningful usable area to the home in six weeks.

"We should have done this ten years ago." - Steve R., Darien

H2: Bathroom Remodeling in Westport, CT

Location: Westport, CT (Fairfield County)
Services: Full Bathroom Remodel, Walk-In Shower Conversion
Timeline: 4 weeks
Contact: (203) 919-9616

Jennifer M.'s Westport primary bathroom had a tub-and-shower combination that hadn't been updated in over two decades. The existing layout was functional but the fixtures, tile, and storage were all dated and difficult to maintain. The scope included a full gut renovation: removing all existing tile, fixtures, and the original tub, and replacing everything with a walk-in shower with full-height tile surround, a new double vanity, updated lighting, and new flooring.

Tile selections and fixture choices were finalized before construction began, which kept the four-week construction timeline clean with no material delays. The walk-in shower required a change to the plumbing rough-in, which was scoped in the proposal and permitted with the rest of the work. The finished bathroom retained the original footprint but used every square foot more effectively through a reconfigured layout.

"Four weeks of construction, and now I have the bathroom I've been dreaming about." - Jennifer M., Westport

H2: Kitchen Remodeling in New Canaan, CT

Location: New Canaan, CT (Fairfield County)
Services: Kitchen Remodeling, Flooring, Load-Bearing Wall Removal, Structural Beam Installation
Timeline: 10 weeks
Contact: (203) 919-9616

The Chen family's New Canaan kitchen was separated from the living and dining areas by a load-bearing wall that divided the main floor into disconnected spaces. The goal was an open-concept layout, which required engineering, permitting, and the installation of a structural beam to carry the load of the wall being removed. We worked with a licensed structural engineer on the beam design and specifications, coordinated permit approval before any work began, and managed the temporary support structure required during the load transfer.

With the structural work complete, the rest of the kitchen renovation proceeded: new cabinetry, countertops, tile backsplash, updated electrical and lighting, and new flooring throughout the main floor. The load-bearing wall removal was the most complex element of the project, but it was also what made the finished space work. The entire project ran 10 weeks from construction start to final walkthrough.

"BuiltWell made it straightforward. Now we can't imagine how we lived before." - The Chens, New Canaan

H2: Kitchen Remodeling and Flooring in Milford, CT

Location: Milford, CT (New Haven County)
Services: Full Kitchen Remodel, LVP Flooring Installation
Timeline: 6 weeks
Contact: (203) 466-9148

Ivana P.'s Milford kitchen was due for a full renovation: cabinets, countertops, appliances, backsplash, and layout adjustments to improve how the space functioned for daily cooking and family use. The project also extended to LVP flooring installed throughout the main floor, replacing older flooring that had worn inconsistently across different rooms. Doing both scopes together allowed us to coordinate the flooring installation around cabinet delivery and installation without requiring the homeowner to manage two separate projects.

All selections (cabinet style, countertop material, backsplash tile, hardware, and LVP color) were finalized before construction began. Construction started on schedule and ran six weeks without delays. Daily updates kept Ivana informed throughout, and the job site was cleaned every evening. The result was a fully updated kitchen and consistent flooring throughout the main living areas, completed under one contract.

"They showed up when they said they would, cleaned up every day, and the kitchen turned out better than I imagined." - Ivana P., Milford
