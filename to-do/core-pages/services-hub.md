H1:
Home Remodeling Services in Connecticut

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Intro
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

We provide 11 home remodeling services for homeowners throughout Fairfield County and New Haven County, Connecticut. Our work covers everything from kitchen and bathroom remodels to basement finishing, flooring, home additions, and accessibility improvements. Every project, large or small, follows the same structured process, the same licensed crew, and the same standard of work.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Primary Services
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H2: Primary Services

Our four primary services are the most commonly requested remodeling scopes we handle, and they're the areas where we've built the deepest track record. Each of these services has dedicated service-and-town pages if you want to explore how we work in your specific town.

H3: Kitchen Remodeling

[Link: /kitchen-remodeling/]

The kitchen is often the most-used room in the house, and it's consistently one of the most involved remodeling projects in terms of trades, sequencing, and finishes. We handle the full scope of [kitchen remodeling](/kitchen-remodeling/): cabinet layout and installation, countertop fabrication, tile work, lighting, plumbing rough-in and trim, electrical, and appliance coordination. Most kitchen remodels in Connecticut fall between $25,000 and $150,000 or more, depending on the size of the kitchen, the quality of materials, and how much of the existing layout changes. Timeline runs six to twelve weeks from demolition to final walkthrough for a full kitchen remodel.

H3: Bathroom Remodeling

[Link: /bathroom-remodeling/]

Whether you're updating a dated hall bath or rebuilding a primary suite bathroom from the studs, our [bathroom remodeling](/bathroom-remodeling/) work covers tile, waterproofing, fixtures, vanities, shower systems, lighting, and ventilation. Bathrooms require careful sequencing because several trades have to work in a tight space in the right order; we manage all of it. Costs typically range from $15,000 to $120,000 or more based on the size of the space and the materials selected. Most bathroom projects run three to six weeks, though timelines can extend for larger primary suites.

H3: Basement Finishing

[Link: /basement-finishing/]

A finished basement adds usable square footage without the complexity of building an addition. Our [basement finishing](/basement-finishing/) work covers framing, insulation, drywall, flooring, lighting, electrical, and in many cases bathroom rough-in or full bathroom builds. We also handle egress window installation when the existing windows don't meet code for the planned use. Finished basement projects in Connecticut typically run from $25,000 to $100,000 or more depending on the size of the space, the level of finish, and whether plumbing is involved. Timeline is generally four to eight weeks.

H3: Flooring Installation

[Link: /flooring/]

We install hardwood, engineered hardwood, luxury vinyl plank, tile, and carpet throughout Fairfield County and New Haven County. [Flooring installation](/flooring/) is often combined with a larger remodel but we also take on standalone flooring projects. Installed cost typically runs from $4 to $25 per square foot depending on the material and the condition of the existing subfloor. Most flooring projects complete in two to five days, with larger or more complex jobs running longer.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Additional Services
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H2: Additional Services

Beyond our four primary services, we offer seven additional remodeling and improvement services that we handle as standalone projects or as part of a larger scope. These services are available to homeowners across both counties.

H3: Home Additions

[Link: /home-additions/]

When you need more space and moving isn't the right answer, a [home addition](/home-additions/) gives you room to grow within your existing property. We build single-story and second-story additions, sunrooms, garage conversions, and bump-outs, handling structural work, roofing tie-in, siding, insulation, and all interior finishes. Addition costs in Connecticut typically run from $150 to $400 per square foot of added space depending on complexity, and most additions take eight to sixteen weeks from start to finish.

H3: Interior Painting

[Link: /interior-painting/]

Our [interior painting](/interior-painting/) work covers walls, ceilings, trim, doors, and built-ins. We use professional-grade paints and prepare every surface properly before we apply anything, filling, sanding, and priming where needed. Interior painting runs from roughly $3 to $6 per square foot of wall surface depending on the number of rooms, ceiling height, and the condition of the existing surfaces. Most interior painting projects complete in two to five days.

H3: Interior Carpentry

[Link: /interior-carpentry/]

We build and install custom millwork, built-in cabinetry, wainscoting, crown molding, coffered ceilings, closet systems, and finish trim throughout the home. [Interior carpentry](/interior-carpentry/) is often what separates a well-finished remodel from one that looks like a renovation. Rates run from $75 to $150 per hour depending on the complexity of the work, and timelines vary widely based on scope.

H3: Attic Conversions

[Link: /attic-conversions/]

Converting an unfinished attic into usable living space (a bedroom, a home office, or a playroom) requires structural assessment, insulation, HVAC extension, electrical, and finished surfaces that work within the angles of the roofline. We handle [attic conversions](/attic-conversions/) from the engineering review through the final finish. Costs typically range from $50,000 to $150,000 depending on the size of the space, the amount of structural work required, and the level of finish. Timeline is six to twelve weeks.

H3: Decks and Porches

[Link: /decks-porches/]

We design and build [decks and porches](/decks-porches/) in pressure-treated lumber, composite decking, and hardwood, including covered porches, screened-in structures, pergolas, and multi-level decks. All work is permitted and built to Connecticut code. Projects range from $15,000 to $75,000 depending on size and materials. Most deck and porch builds complete in two to four weeks once materials are on site.

H3: Remodeling Design and Planning

[Link: /remodeling-design-planning/]

Before construction begins, a well-thought-out plan saves time, money, and rework. Our [remodeling design and planning](/remodeling-design-planning/) service covers space planning, material selection, finish coordination, and project documentation. We work with your existing layout or help you think through changes to it before any permits are pulled or contracts are signed. Design and planning fees typically run from $2,500 to $15,000 depending on the complexity of the project. Most design phases take two to six weeks.

H3: Comfort and Accessibility Remodeling

[Link: /comfort-accessibility-remodeling/]

We make homes safer and easier to navigate for people of all ages and abilities. Our [comfort and accessibility remodeling](/comfort-accessibility-remodeling/) work includes grab bar and handrail installation, roll-in shower conversions, threshold removal, wider doorways, ramp installation, and first-floor bedroom and bathroom adaptations. These projects range from $5,000 to $50,000 depending on what's involved, and most complete in one to four weeks.
