H2: Schedule a Free Consultation

Every project starts with a conversation. Our consultations are completely free and carry no obligation; we want to understand your project before either of us makes any commitments. We offer two ways to connect, so you can choose whichever works best for your schedule and situation.

Both options are available to homeowners across Fairfield County and New Haven County.

H3: In-Person Consultation

For most remodeling projects, meeting at your home is the most useful first step. We come to you, walk through the space, take note of what you're working with, and talk through what you have in mind. Seeing the actual conditions (the layout, the finishes, the structure) lets us give you a more accurate picture of scope and timing than any phone call could. In-person appointments are booked through Google Calendar Appointment Scheduling. Select a time that works for you, and we'll confirm the appointment and arrive ready to take a real look.

H3: Remote Consultation

If you're in the early stages of planning, working through ideas before you're ready for a site visit, or simply want to have a first conversation before committing to an in-person meeting, a remote consultation works well. We meet over Google Meet or Zoom, you can share photos or drawings, and we can talk through feasibility, rough timelines, and what questions you should be thinking about. Remote appointments are booked through Calendly and are a good fit for homeowners who want to get a sense of the process before scheduling a visit. Both Fairfield County and New Haven County homeowners are welcome to start here.

H2: Call or Text Us

Calling or texting is the fastest way to reach us when you have a specific question or want to talk through something before booking. We're available Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm, and Saturday from 8am to 3pm. Texts are welcome.

Fairfield County: (203) 919-9616
New Haven County: (203) 466-9148

If you're not sure which number to use, call or text either one; both reach our team, and we'll make sure your message gets to the right person.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
What to Expect After You Reach Out
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

H2: What to Expect After You Reach Out

We keep the process straightforward from the moment you contact us. Here's how it typically goes:

You'll hear back from us within one business day of your initial contact. If you reach out over the weekend or after hours, we'll follow up the next business morning.

Once we've connected, we work to schedule your consultation within the week. Most homeowners are able to get on our calendar within a few days of that first conversation.

After the consultation, whether in-person or remote, we put together a written estimate for the project. You can expect that within three to five business days following your consultation. The estimate is detailed enough to be useful, and it gives you a clear basis for comparison if you're gathering multiple bids.

There's no pressure at any point in this process. Reaching out doesn't obligate you to anything, and neither does the consultation or the estimate. We'd rather you take the time to feel confident about the decision than feel rushed into something. If you have questions about how we work before you reach out, the [Our Process](/process/) and [FAQ](/faq/) pages are a good place to start.
