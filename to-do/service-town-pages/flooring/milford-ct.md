H2: Milford Flooring Conditions
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Milford's mix of coastal and inland neighborhoods creates two distinct sets of conditions for flooring decisions. Homes within two to three blocks of Long Island Sound or the Housatonic River experience elevated ambient humidity year-round. H2: Flooring Questions
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

### What is the best flooring for a Connecticut home?

The best flooring depends on the room, the level of the home, and the moisture conditions at your property. For above-grade living rooms and bedrooms in inland homes, solid hardwood is a durable choice when properly acclimated. For coastal areas with elevated year-round humidity, engineered hardwood or luxury vinyl plank is the better choice even above grade. For basements, luxury vinyl plank is the most reliable option statewide. For kitchens and entryways, LVP and tile handle regular moisture well. For bedrooms above grade, carpet is appropriate. We assess the specific conditions at your home and recommend accordingly.

### How long does flooring installation take?

Most residential flooring projects complete in two to five days. A single room takes one to two days. Multi-room installations covering 250 to 600 square feet take two to three days. Full-floor or whole-home installations covering 600 to 1,200 square feet take three to five days. If you're installing solid hardwood, add three to five days for the required acclimation period. When flooring is part of a larger renovation such as a kitchen remodel, the flooring timeline is built into the overall project schedule.

### Can new flooring be installed over existing flooring?

In some cases, yes. Luxury vinyl plank and engineered hardwood can often be installed directly over existing hard surfaces when the subfloor is level, structurally sound, and within acceptable moisture ranges. We assess each situation individually. In older Milford homes with multiple layers of flooring built up over the years, the added height can create threshold and door clearance issues that need to be addressed. When overlay installation is appropriate, it saves removal cost and time. When it is not, we explain the reasons clearly before any work begins.

### What is the difference between solid and engineered hardwood?

Solid hardwood is milled from a single piece of wood and can be sanded and refinished multiple times over its lifetime. It performs best in above-grade rooms with stable humidity. Engineered hardwood has a real wood veneer bonded over a plywood core, which makes it significantly more stable through the humidity changes Connecticut homes experience seasonally. In Milford's coastal neighborhoods, engineered hardwood is the appropriate choice over solid even in living areas above grade, because ambient humidity from Long Island Sound is elevated enough to cause problems with solid wood over time. The right choice depends on where the flooring is going and what the local conditions are.

### Do I need to move my furniture before flooring installation?

We move standard furniture within the work area as part of the installation: sofas, chairs, tables, beds, dressers, and similar household items. Heavy or specialty pieces such as grand pianos or large gun safes require specialty movers, which we can help coordinate in advance. Appliance disconnection and reconnection is included when flooring extends through kitchen areas. Anything wall-mounted or built in should be discussed at the consultation so we can plan the scope accordingly.
