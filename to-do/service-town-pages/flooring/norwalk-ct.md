H2: What Is Included in Flooring Installation

A flooring installation by BuiltWell covers subfloor assessment and preparation, removal and disposal of existing flooring, underlayment, installation to manufacturer specifications, transition strips, base shoe or quarter-round molding, and full cleanup.

We start every flooring project with a thorough look at what is underneath. In Norwalk's Victorian-era homes and older colonials, squeaky subfloors are one of the most common conditions we find. Squeaks come from subfloor boards that have loosened over time, the fix is screwing the subfloor down to the joists before new flooring is installed. This is standard practice on our end. If the subfloor is not tightened first, the squeak transfers directly through the new floor, and no amount of quality material overhead resolves the problem underneath.

In homes built during the 1960s and 1970s: a common housing type in Norwalk's inland neighborhoods, original vinyl composition tile or resilient sheet goods may be present under existing flooring. If that material was manufactured before 1980, it may contain asbestos. We do not disturb suspected asbestos-containing materials. If we find what we believe could be vinyl asbestos tile during the removal process, we stop work in that area, inform you directly, and the material requires testing and licensed abatement before flooring work continues. This is not a situation we take shortcuts around.

Coastal proximity near Long Island Sound and the Five Mile River means humidity variation in Norwalk is more pronounced than in inland Connecticut towns. Solid hardwood installed in a coastal Norwalk home needs a minimum of five to seven days of on-site acclimation before installation begins. We schedule this into the project before the installation date, acclimation happens during the project window, not as a delay once the crew is on-site.

H2: Flooring Questions

### What is the best flooring for a Connecticut home?

The right flooring depends on the room, the level of the home, and the moisture conditions at your property. Solid hardwood performs well above grade in living rooms and bedrooms when properly acclimated, in coastal Norwalk, allow five to seven days minimum. LVP is the most reliable choice for below-grade spaces and for whole-home installations in homes with humidity variation. Tile works well in kitchens, bathrooms, and entryways. For Norwalk homes, LVP is frequently the most practical choice for whole-floor projects because it handles the coastal humidity without extended acclimation requirements.

### How long does flooring installation take?

Most flooring projects complete in two to five days. A single room takes one to two days. Multi-room installations covering 250 to 600 square feet typically take two to three days. Whole-home installations take three to five days. Solid hardwood in a coastal Norwalk home requires five to seven days of acclimation on-site before installation begins, we schedule this into the project so it does not add delay once the crew is on-site. If subfloor repair is needed, that adds time at the front of the project, which we account for in the proposal timeline.

### Can new flooring be installed over existing flooring?

In some cases, yes. LVP and engineered hardwood can be installed over existing hard surfaces if the subfloor is level, structurally sound, and within acceptable moisture parameters. For Norwalk homes with older flooring, we assess whether the existing material could be vinyl asbestos tile before recommending overlay installation. If the original material is confirmed clear, overlay may be appropriate depending on height and levelness. We do not cover up problems, and we assess each situation on its own conditions before making a recommendation.

### What is the difference between solid and engineered hardwood?

Solid hardwood is milled from a single piece of wood and can be sanded and refinished multiple times over its life. It performs best above grade in rooms with stable humidity, in coastal Norwalk, proper acclimation of five to seven days is required before installation. Engineered hardwood has a real wood veneer bonded over a plywood core, making it significantly more resistant to the expansion and contraction that coastal humidity variation causes in solid wood. Engineered hardwood is the better choice in Norwalk's moisture-affected areas, below-grade spaces, and rooms with pronounced seasonal humidity swings.

### Do I need to move my furniture before flooring installation?

We move standard furniture within the work area as part of the project: sofas, tables, beds, dressers, and similar household items. Exceptionally heavy or specialty pieces require separate coordination that we can help arrange in advance. In Victorian-era homes with tighter hallways and doorways, we plan furniture moves carefully at the start of the project. We'll confirm exactly what we handle at the proposal stage so there are no surprises on installation day.
