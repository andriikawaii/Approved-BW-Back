## What Is Included in a Flooring Installation

A complete flooring installation in Darien covers subfloor assessment and preparation, removal and disposal of existing flooring, appropriate underlayment for the material selected, the installation itself to manufacturer specifications, transition strips between rooms and flooring types, and base shoe or quarter-round molding where the floor meets the baseboard.

We start every project with a thorough subfloor assessment. In Darien, that assessment includes moisture testing in any below-grade or slab-on-grade space, a check for level and structural integrity, and a specific look for any irregularity that indicates ledge rock was removed during original construction. Homes where ledge removal left an uneven concrete slab require self-leveling compound or grinding before any floating floor can go down. We identify that before the proposal, not after installation has started.

Where original hardwood floors are present under carpet, we assess whether sanding and refinishing is the right path before recommending replacement. In Darien's 1950s through 1980s housing stock, the original 2.25-inch strip hardwood is often sound and worth restoring for architectural consistency. New material costs more and does not always produce a better result in these homes.

## Flooring Considerations Specific to Darien

Darien's geology and housing stock create flooring conditions that are specific to this town and that not every contractor accounts for.

The high water table in several Darien neighborhoods means that below-grade and slab-on-grade spaces require moisture testing before any flooring product is selected. Luxury vinyl plank is the appropriate wood-look option for these areas. Solid hardwood is not suitable at or below grade in Darien's conditions regardless of how dry the slab appears at the time of installation. We test before we specify, and we hold to that position even when a client requests solid hardwood in a space where the conditions do not support it.

Darien's mudrooms and kitchen areas are high-traffic zones. Families here track in moisture, soil, and wear year-round. For these spaces, we recommend luxury vinyl plank with a wear layer rated at 12 mil or above. Products below that rating show wear faster under the daily use these areas receive.

Darien clients expect contractors to remove shoes on entry, use floor protection throughout the home, and clean up completely at the end of every workday. We work that way on every project.

## Flooring Questions

### What is the best flooring for a Connecticut home?

The best flooring depends on the room, the level of the home, and the moisture conditions at your property. For above-grade living rooms and bedrooms, solid hardwood is a reliable choice when properly acclimated. For kitchens, mudrooms, and high-traffic areas, luxury vinyl plank or tile handles moisture and wear well. For basements and below-grade spaces in Connecticut, luxury vinyl plank is the appropriate choice. It is waterproof, stable through humidity swings, and durable under daily use.

### How long does flooring installation take?

Most flooring projects in Darien complete in two to five days. A single room takes one to two days. Multi-room installations covering 250 to 600 square feet take two to three days. Full-floor installations take three to five days. Projects requiring concrete leveling or significant subfloor repair may extend to seven to ten days. Solid hardwood also requires three to five days of on-site acclimation before installation begins.

### Can new flooring be installed over existing flooring?

In some situations, yes. Luxury vinyl plank and engineered hardwood can be installed over existing hard surfaces if the subfloor is level, structurally sound, and within acceptable moisture parameters. We assess each situation individually. If overlay installation is appropriate, it saves removal time and cost. If the existing surface is damaged, uneven, or has moisture issues, removal is the right path and we explain why.

### What is the difference between solid and engineered hardwood?

Solid hardwood is milled from a single piece of wood and can be sanded and refinished multiple times over its life. It is appropriate for above-grade rooms with stable humidity. Engineered hardwood uses a real wood veneer over a plywood core. The cross-layer construction makes it more resistant to expansion and contraction from humidity changes, which is why it is the right choice for below-grade spaces, rooms over concrete, and areas where seasonal moisture swings are significant. For Darien homes with radiant heat, engineered hardwood at 3/8-inch maximum thickness or luxury vinyl plank rated for radiant applications is the appropriate choice.

### Do I need to move my furniture before flooring installation?

We move standard furniture within the work area as part of the installation. That includes sofas, chairs, tables, beds, and dressers. Heavy or specialty items such as grand pianos or large antique pieces require specialty movers, which we can help coordinate in advance. Appliances in kitchen areas are disconnected and reconnected as part of the scope when flooring extends there. We confirm exactly what we will handle and what requires separate coordination at the proposal stage.
