H2: What Is Included in a Kitchen Remodel in Stamford
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A full kitchen remodel in Stamford covers every trade from demolition through final paint: cabinetry, countertops, tile, flooring, electrical, plumbing, appliances, permits, and daily communication throughout construction.

We begin with demolition and a thorough assessment of existing conditions. In Stamford, that assessment often reveals one or more of the following: 100-amp electrical panels in 1950s-to-1960s construction that cannot support modern kitchen loads, original plumbing configurations in pre-war homes that need updating before new fixtures can be set, water damage inside wall cavities from slow leaks, or plaster walls in older properties that require different patching and finish techniques than standard drywall. We identify all of this before you commit to a scope and before construction begins.

New cabinetry installation includes soft-close hardware and adjustable shelving as standard. Custom cabinetry lead times in Connecticut run eight to twelve weeks; semi-custom runs four to six weeks. We order early in the planning phase so cabinet delivery aligns with your scheduled construction start. Countertop fabrication and installation, backsplash tile, new flooring with proper underlayment, and interior painting within the kitchen space are all included. Electrical scope covers panel assessment and upgrade where required, new circuits for appliances, under-cabinet lighting, and updated outlet and switch locations. Plumbing includes sink and dishwasher connections, garbage disposal, and relocated water lines where applicable. Appliance coordination and installation are included so you are not managing that separately.

For condo projects in Stamford, we handle the additional coordination layer: building management approval, elevator reservations, noise hour compliance, and staging of materials and debris in ways that protect building common areas. These logistics are built into the project schedule from the start, not treated as afterthoughts.

Permits from the Stamford Building Department (separate building, electrical, and plumbing permits where required) are handled by us. We coordinate all inspections. Structural work that requires stamped plans is coordinated with a CT-licensed engineer. Daily cleanup throughout construction and a final walkthrough close the project.

Yes. The Stamford Building Department issues separate permits for building, electrical, and plumbing work. Condo renovations also require building management or HOA approval before permit applications can be submitted. We coordinate all permit applications, stamped plans where needed, and every required inspection. You do not need to manage that process independently.

Condo projects in Stamford may have longer scheduling windows due to building management coordination and noise hour restrictions.
