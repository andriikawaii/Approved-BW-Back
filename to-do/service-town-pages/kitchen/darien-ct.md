──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Most kitchen remodeling projects in Darien require separate building, electrical, and plumbing permits, each issued by the Darien Building Department and each requiring its own inspections.

The building permit covers structural and general construction work. The electrical permit covers new circuits, panel upgrades, and any changes to the electrical system. If the project includes moving or adding plumbing fixtures, a separate plumbing permit is required. Any structural modification (removing or altering a load-bearing wall, adding a structural beam, or modifying the foundation) requires plans stamped by a Connecticut-licensed architect or structural engineer before the building permit will be issued.

We handle all permit applications and coordinate every required inspection as part of the project. You do not manage any part of that process. We build permit review time into the project schedule during planning, so permitting delays do not catch anyone off guard. If your project requires stamped structural plans, we coordinate with a licensed engineer and include that in the scope.

Yes. The Darien Building Department issues separate building, electrical, and plumbing permits for kitchen remodeling projects, and each requires its own inspections. We handle all permit applications and inspection coordination. You do not manage any part of that process. Permit review time is built into the project schedule during planning.

### How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Darien?

Kitchen remodeling in Darien ranges from approximately $40,000 for a minor refresh (cabinet doors, countertops, appliances, paint) to $200,000 or more for a fully custom renovation with structural changes and premium materials. Mid-range projects fall between $80,000 and $140,000. An important factor in Darien's older housing stock: 1960s and 1970s homes commonly have 100-amp electrical panels. Once walls are open for a kitchen renovation, upgrading to 200-amp service is a code requirement when the existing panel is undersized for modern kitchen loads. We assess panel capacity during planning and include the upgrade cost in the written scope when it applies.

### How long does a kitchen remodel take in Darien?

Active construction on a Darien kitchen remodel runs six to twelve weeks depending on scope. Custom cabinet orders add eight to twelve weeks of lead time before construction starts; semi-custom cabinets add four to six weeks. We finalize cabinet selections early in the planning phase and order immediately so the lead time runs alongside pre-construction work. Permit review time is also built into the schedule. A mid-range project from signed proposal to final walkthrough typically runs twelve to eighteen weeks total when you account for cabinet lead time and permit review.

### My Darien home has ledge rock. How does that affect a kitchen addition or expansion?

If your project involves any below-grade work, a kitchen addition with new footings, or drainage modifications, ledge rock is a real planning factor. Darien has significant ledge rock conditions throughout the town. When ledge is encountered during excavation, hydraulic hammering is required. It is the only permitted method in most situations, and it is slower and more expensive than excavating soil. We assess site conditions before any scope is finalized and disclose the contingency range in writing so you can plan the budget accurately. We do not finalize scope on addition or expansion projects without a clear-eyed assessment of what is underground.
