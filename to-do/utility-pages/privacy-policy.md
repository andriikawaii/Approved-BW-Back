H1:
Privacy Policy

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Intro
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Last Updated: March 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Legacy of Clean LLC d/b/a BuiltWell CT ("BuiltWell CT," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information provided by visitors to buildwellct.com and through our contact and booking forms.

We operate as a licensed home remodeling contractor serving Fairfield County and New Haven County, Connecticut. CT Home Improvement Contractor License #0668405.

By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not submit your information through this site.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
H2: Information We Collect
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
We collect information only when you voluntarily provide it through our contact forms, consultation booking forms, or texting widget. The information we collect includes:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your phone number
- Your home address or project location (when scheduling an on-site visit)
- The nature of your remodeling project (type, scope, timeline, budget range)
- Any additional details you include in free-text message fields

We do not collect payment card information through this website. Payment processing for contracted work is handled separately and in accordance with the terms of your written service agreement.

We also collect standard web analytics data automatically when you visit our site, including your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and time spent on each page. This data is collected through Google Analytics and does not personally identify you unless combined with information you submit directly.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
H2: How We Use Your Information
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
We use the information you provide solely to:

- Respond to your inquiry or consultation request
- Prepare and deliver an estimate for your project
- Schedule and confirm on-site or remote consultations
- Communicate about your project before, during, and after construction
- Send project updates, inspection notices, or documentation required for your remodel

We do not sell your information. We do not rent your information. We do not share your information with marketing companies, lead aggregators, or third-party advertisers.

We use aggregate, anonymized analytics data to understand how visitors use our website and to improve our content. This data cannot be used to identify you.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
H2: Third-Party Services We Use
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
To operate this website and provide our services, we use the following third-party tools. Each operates under its own privacy policy.

Google Analytics
We use Google Analytics to understand website traffic patterns. Google Analytics collects anonymized data about your visit. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Google Calendar
If you book an in-person consultation, scheduling is managed through Google Calendar Appointment Scheduling. Your name, email, and requested appointment time are shared with Google to complete the booking. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Calendly
If you book a remote consultation (video call), scheduling is managed through Calendly. Your name, email, and selected time slot are shared with Calendly to generate your appointment confirmation and video link. Calendly's privacy policy is available at calendly.com/privacy.

Texting Widget
This website includes a custom texting widget that connects you directly with our team. Messages sent through this widget are received and managed by us. Standard message and data rates may apply based on your mobile carrier.

We do not use Facebook Pixel, retargeting networks, or advertising data brokers on this website.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
H2: AI-Generated Content and Images
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Some of the written content on this website was produced with the assistance of artificial intelligence writing tools and reviewed by our team before publication. AI assistance does not affect the accuracy of the information presented; all service descriptions, pricing ranges, timelines, and local details reflect our actual work and operating conditions.

Some images on this website are AI-generated and are used for illustrative purposes only. They are not photographs of actual completed BuiltWell CT projects. Where images depict completed projects, this is noted in the accompanying content. Actual project photos, where used, are from real projects performed by BuiltWell CT in Connecticut.

All customer testimonials displayed on this website are from real customers who engaged our services. No testimonials or customer reviews have been generated, fabricated, or modified using AI. This practice is consistent with the Federal Trade Commission's Final Rule on Consumer Reviews and Testimonials (effective October 21, 2024) and the FTC's Endorsement Guides.

We do not use personal data collected through this website to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. We do not sell customer data to any party for AI training purposes.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
H2: Cookies
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This website uses cookies (small text files stored in your browser) to support website functionality and analytics.

Functional cookies allow the website to operate correctly, including remembering your booking selections and form progress.

Analytics cookies, placed by Google Analytics, collect anonymized data about how you navigate the site. These cookies do not identify you personally.

Most browsers allow you to control or disable cookies through their settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain features of this website, including the booking widget.

We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking cookies.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
H2: Data Retention
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
We retain your contact information and project inquiry details for as long as is reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and, if you become a client, to manage and document your project. For completed projects, we retain records in accordance with Connecticut contractor licensing and business record requirements, which may require retention of records for up to seven years.

If you would like your information removed from our records and you are not a current or past client with an active contractual relationship, contact us at the address below and we will honor that request within a reasonable timeframe.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
H2: Children's Privacy
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through this site, contact us immediately and we will delete it.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
H2: Your Rights
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
You have the right to:

- Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your information (subject to our legal retention obligations)
- Withdraw consent to future communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below. We will respond within 30 days.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
H2: Security
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
We take reasonable measures to protect the information you submit through this website, including the use of HTTPS encryption on all pages. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted through our website.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
H2: Changes to This Policy
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of this website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
